A Minor Pentatonic Scale

The minor pentatonic scale is one of the most versatile and commonly used scales in rock, blues, and pop music. This "Box 1" pattern at the 5th fret is the foundation for countless guitar solos.

Scale Notes

A C D E G

Intervals

1 (Root) ♭3 (Minor 3rd) 4 (Perfect 4th) 5 (Perfect 5th) ♭7 (Minor 7th)

Common Uses

  • Blues improvisation
  • Rock guitar solos
  • Creating melodic riffs
  • Jamming over A minor, C major, or related progressions

Practice Tips

  1. Start by playing the scale ascending (low E to high E) and descending
  2. Use a metronome and gradually increase speed
  3. Practice with backing tracks in A minor
  4. Try connecting this pattern to other pentatonic boxes
